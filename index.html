Sail Race

Race a solar sail through gravity and light. No reflexes needed: pause and time warp are free, only the path counts.

Speed

Layout Viewpoint of the selected view
Arrow keys: up/down first angle, left/right second angle, Shift for 5°, Space to pause

3D views: click a view to select it, drag to rotate, wheel to zoom. Keys 1 to 5 jump the selected view to 3D, top, side, orbit plane or the light side, V cycles the layout, F follows the sail, [ and ] change speed. The red line is the sail normal, the yellow line points to the light. The green dashed line shows where you go if you change nothing. Switching the control scheme restarts the level and keeps separate best times.
