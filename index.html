    How to play

    Sunlight pushes a sail, gravity pulls it. Tilt the sail to steer.
    1. Light pushes the sail along the red arrow, away from the light. Gravity pulls toward the Sun or planet.
    2. Tilt the sail with the pad: right leans it forward along the orbit, up leans it toward the orbit pole. The green dot is the guide.
    3. There are no reflexes to test. Pause and slow down whenever you like. The best time wins, and every run can be replayed.
